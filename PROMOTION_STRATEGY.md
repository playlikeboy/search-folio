# Promotion Strategy & Channels 📣

Here is a curated list of communities where you can post about SearchFolio.

## 🔴 Reddit Strategy

*Tip: Don't just "dump a link". Redditors hate spam. Write a story or ask for feedback.*

### Top Tier (High Traffic)
1.  **r/webdev** (1.8M+ Members)
    *   **When**: "Showoff Saturday" (Strict rule: Showoff posts ONLY on Saturdays).
    *   **Angle**: "I built a portfolio that looks exactly like Google Search using Next.js 15."
2.  **r/reactjs** (380k+ Members)
    *   **When**: Look for "Showcase" flair or monthly threads.
    *   **Angle**: Focus on the technical stack (Next.js 15, Tailwind 4, Framer Motion).
3.  **r/nextjs** (80k+ Members)
    *   **When**: Anytime (Check rules).
    *   **Angle**: "Open Source Template: Next.js 15 + Static Export portfolio."
4.  **r/SideProject** (140k+ Members)
    *   **When**: Anytime.
    *   **Angle**: "I made a side project to help devs get hired faster." (This is a very supportive community).

### Niche / Feedback (High Engagment)
5.  **r/roastmyportfolio**
    *   **Angle**: "Roast my Google-themed portfolio. Is it too gimmicky?" (Controversy drives views).
6.  **r/frontend**
    *   **Angle**: "Recreating the Google UI in pure Tailwind CSS 4."
7.  **r/design_critique**
    *   **Angle**: "Does this look authentic enough? Replicating Google's UX."

## ✍️ Article Platforms (Republishing)

*Tip: Post on your main blog first (if you have one), then "Canonical Link" to these platforms to boost SEO.*

1.  **Dev.to** (You have the draft for this!)
    *   *Best for:* General developer audience, huge viral potential.
2.  **Hashnode**
    *   *Best for:* Personal branding, deeper technical community.
    *   *Strategy:* Copy your Dev.to article but change the title slightly.
3.  **Medium** (Submit to Publications)
    *   *Target Publications:* "Level Up Coding", "JavaScript in Plain English".
    *   *Strategy:* Requires high quality. Focus on the "Career/Hiring" aspect.
4.  **IndieHackers**
    *   *Best for:* The "Business" side.
    *   *Angle:* "How I launched my first Gumroad product (SearchFolio)." Focus on the sales/marketing journey.

## 🍊 Hacker News (Show HN)

*High Difficulty / High Reward*

*   **Url**: `news.ycombinator.com`
*   **Title Format**: `Show HN: SearchFolio – A developer portfolio that looks like Google Search`
*   **Timing**: Post on a weekday morning (8am - 10am PT).
*   **Warning**: HN users are critical. Be humble in the comments. If you hit the front page, expect thousands of visitors in an hour.
